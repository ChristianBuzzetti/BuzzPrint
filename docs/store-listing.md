- **Restricted capability `runFullTrust` justification:** BuzzPrint is a desktop (WPF) screen capture and recording tool. It needs full trust to register global keyboard shortcuts (Print Screen, Ctrl+Alt+R), draw a capture overlay over every monitor, capture the screen and system audio (WASAPI loopback), run the bundled FFmpeg process to encode recordings, and live in the system tray.

## English (en-US)

**Product name:** BuzzPrint

**Short description (≤ 100 chars):**
Screen capture, recording and step-by-step docs for Windows, with a live companion.

**Description:**
BuzzPrint makes screenshots and screen recordings fast to take, easy to find and ready to share.

Capture a region, a window or the full screen with Print Screen. Record video with system audio and your microphone, or audio only. Annotate with arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, highlighter and pixelate, and keep the original untouched.

Every capture lands in a searchable library with Today, Pinned, Edited and Recordings tabs, day filters and your own sections. Open the quick menu from anywhere to paste any capture into the app you're using.

Turn a set of captures into a document in seconds: export them, in order, with titles and notes, to PowerPoint, Word, PDF or one tall image. Perfect for how-to guides, bug reports and runbooks.

Make it yours with 23 palettes, fonts and density options, and meet Boberto, an animated companion you can dress up in Boberto Studio.

Everything stays on your PC: no account, no telemetry, no ads. English and Spanish built in. Open source (MIT).

**Features (one per line, ≤ 200 chars each):**
- Capture region, window or full screen with Print Screen
- Record MP4 video with system audio and microphone, or MP3 audio only
- Annotate: arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, highlighter, pixelate, crop
- Library with search, day filters, pins and your own sections
- Quick menu to paste any capture into the app you're using
- Export captures in order to PowerPoint, Word, PDF or one image
- 23 palettes, fonts and an animated companion, Boberto
- No account, no telemetry: everything stays on your PC

**Keywords:** screenshot, screen capture, screen recorder, annotate, snipping, documentation, steps, PowerPoint export
