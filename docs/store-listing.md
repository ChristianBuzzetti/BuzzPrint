## Properties

- **Category:** Productivity. Subcategory: none.
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/ChristianBuzzetti/BuzzPrint/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
- **Website:** https://github.com/ChristianBuzzetti/BuzzPrint
- **Support contact:** https://github.com/ChristianBuzzetti/BuzzPrint/issues
- **Product declarations:** no personal data collected; works with mouse and keyboard; does not depend on other software beyond Windows (WebView2 ships with Windows 11).
- **System requirements:** Windows 10 version 1809 or later, x64; 4 GB RAM recommended.
- **Restricted capability `runFullTrust` justification:** BuzzPrint is a desktop (WPF) screen capture and recording tool. It needs full trust to register global keyboard shortcuts (Print Screen, Ctrl+Alt+R), draw a capture overlay over every monitor, capture the screen and system audio (WASAPI loopback), run the bundled FFmpeg process to encode recordings, and live in the system tray.
- **Age rating questionnaire:** utility app; no user-generated content shared online, no communication between users, no purchases, no location. If asked about AI: yes, optional live generative AI (text about the person's own captures), off by default.
- **Live generative AI (policy 11.16):** in the submission, mark that the product uses live generative AI. It is disclosed in the description below, and every AI window has "Report inappropriate content" (opens a GitHub issue with the `ai-content-report` template).
- **Notes for certification:** AI features are optional and off by default. They need the user's own API key for a provider they choose (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, a local Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible service); BuzzPrint has no server. Everything else works without a key. To try AI without a paid key, use Gemini's free tier or a local Ollama.
- **Pricing:** free. **Markets:** all.

## English (en-US)

**Product name:** BuzzPrint

**Short description (≤ 100 chars):**
Screen capture, recording and step-by-step docs for Windows, with a live companion.

**Description:**
BuzzPrint makes screenshots and screen recordings fast to take, easy to find and ready to share.

Capture a region, a window or the full screen with Print Screen. Record video with system audio and your microphone, or audio only. Annotate with arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, highlighter and pixelate, and keep the original untouched.

Every capture lands in a searchable library with Today, Pinned, Edited and Recordings tabs, day filters and your own sections. Open the quick menu from anywhere to paste any capture into the app you're using.

Turn a set of captures into a document in seconds: export them, in order, with titles and notes, to PowerPoint, Word, PDF or one tall image. Perfect for how-to guides, bug reports and runbooks.

Optional AI, off until you turn it on: connect your own Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter or local Ollama key to get titles and summaries, step-by-step guides, sensitive data hidden in a copy, answers about a capture and incident reports. This content is generated live by the AI service you choose, so it can be wrong; BuzzPrint shows what will be sent before sending it, and you can report inappropriate AI content from any AI window.

Make it yours with 23 palettes, fonts and density options, and meet Boberto, an animated companion you can dress up in Boberto Studio.

No account, no telemetry, no ads: your captures stay on your PC unless you share them or send them to the AI service you set up. English and Spanish built in. Open source (MIT).

**Features (one per line, ≤ 200 chars each):**
- Capture region, window or full screen with Print Screen
- Record MP4 video with system audio and microphone, or MP3 audio only
- Annotate: arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, highlighter, pixelate, crop
- Library with search, day filters, pins and your own sections
- Quick menu to paste any capture into the app you're using
- Export captures in order to PowerPoint, Word, PDF or one image
- Optional generative AI with your own key: titles, summaries, guides, protect data, incident reports
- Search the text inside your captures (OCR, on your PC)
- 23 palettes, fonts and an animated companion, Boberto
- No account, no telemetry: your captures stay on your PC

**Keywords:** screenshot, screen capture, screen recorder, annotate, snipping, documentation, steps, AI

**What's new in this version (0.5.0):** Optional AI features, off until you turn them on with your own API key (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama or any compatible service): title and summary for captures, step-by-step guides, protect sensitive data, ask about a capture, incident reports and organizing the library. Nothing is sent without showing you first. Also new: search the text inside your captures (OCR, on your PC), document templates for exports, an in-app guide and a redesigned look.
